# LustLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

LustLock is a faith-based accountability and recovery app designed to help Christian men resist lust, quit pornography, track progress, journal, pray, and use optional device-level shielding tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LustLock may collect, how it is used, and the choices users have.

## Information We Collect

LustLock may collect the following types of information:

- Account information, such as name, email address, and user ID.
- Subscription information, such as purchase status and entitlement status through Apple and RevenueCat.
- App content entered by the user, such as journal entries, check-ins, prayer messages, reflections, community posts, notes, and recovery-related responses.
- Sensitive recovery and faith-related information that users choose to provide, including information related to lust, pornography recovery, prayer, spiritual goals, and accountability.
- App usage information needed to operate core features, such as streaks, check-ins, relapse logs, program progress, notification preferences, and shield settings.

LustLock does not intentionally collect payment card numbers or banking information. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Provide the onboarding, recovery, journaling, prayer, accountability, and progress tracking features.
- Manage subscriptions and Pro access.
- Save user progress and settings.
- Provide optional Screen Time based app and website shielding tools.
- Send privacy-conscious reminders and notifications when enabled by the user.
- Improve app reliability, security, and functionality.

We do not sell user personal information.

We do not use user recovery content for third-party advertising.

## Subscriptions and Purchases

LustLock uses RevenueCat and Apple In-App Purchase to manage subscriptions. Apple processes payment information, and RevenueCat helps us determine whether a user has an active subscription entitlement.

## Screen Time and Shield Features

LustLock may use Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Screen Time related APIs when users choose to enable blocking or shielding features. These permissions are used to help users select apps or websites to block and display encouragement when blocked content is accessed.

LustLock does not use these permissions for advertising.

## Notifications

LustLock may send notifications if the user grants permission. Notification text is designed to be discreet and avoid explicit recovery language on the lock screen.

Users can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

## Data Sharing

We may use trusted service providers to operate the app, including:

- Supabase for authentication and app data storage.
- RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management.
- Apple for App Store purchases, subscriptions, notifications, and Screen Time permissions.

These providers process information only as needed to provide app functionality.

## Data Retention

We retain user information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may request account or data deletion by contacting us.

## Children's Privacy

LustLock is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## User Choices

Users can:

- Update profile information inside the app.
- Disable notifications in iOS Settings.
- Revoke Screen Time permissions in iOS Settings.
- Cancel subscriptions through Apple subscription settings.
- Request account or data deletion by contacting us.
