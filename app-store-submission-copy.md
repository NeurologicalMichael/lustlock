### Description
LustLock is a faith-based recovery and accountability app built to help Christian men fight lust, quit porn, and pursue lasting freedom with structure, privacy, and daily support.

An auto-renewable subscription purchase is required to access LustLock features after onboarding. Available subscription plans and prices are shown in the app before purchase.

The app combines a focused recovery plan, private progress tracking, emergency support tools, scripture-based encouragement, journaling, and optional device-level shielding features. LustLock is designed for men who are serious about breaking porn habits, resisting temptation, and rebuilding discipline through faith.

Key features include:

- Daily check-ins for mood, energy, and temptation level
- Streak tracking and progress logs
- Private journaling and reflection
- Emergency support button for moments of weakness
- Prayer partner chat for faith-based encouragement
- Scripture and courage prompts
- Screen Time based app and web shielding tools
- Accountability-focused reminders
- Simple home screen centered around immediate help and daily discipline

LustLock helps users identify triggers, interrupt urges, track clean days, and replace porn habits with prayer, scripture, reflection, and action. The goal is simple: help Christian men stop watching porn, resist lust, and stay accountable one decision at a time.

LustLock is built with privacy in mind. Notifications are intentionally discreet, and sensitive recovery language is avoided in lock-screen reminders.

LustLock is not a replacement for therapy, pastoral care, crisis support, or professional medical treatment. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.

## Build Upload

You do not paste an app file into this page.

Upload the build from Xcode:

1. Open `/Users/iphonecool/lustlock/ios/LustLock.xcworkspace`
2. Select a real iOS device or "Any iOS Device"
3. Choose `Product > Archive`
4. When Organizer opens, choose `Distribute App`
5. Select `App Store Connect`
6. Upload
7. Wait for processing
8. Return to this App Store Connect version page and select the processed build in the Build section

After a build is selected, attach the first subscriptions to this same app version before clicking Add for Review.
